      How the picks are made

      Trained on23,955 internationals
      Holdout accuracy60.0%
      Elo-only baseline60.5%
      Data frozen27 Jun 2026
      ModelsXGBoost · Poisson · Shootout

      An XGBoost model predicts a win, draw or loss for every match. When a draw is the most likely result, a separate penalty-shootout model picks who goes through. Poisson regressors estimate the goals.

      Everything is frozen at the end of the group stage, so every knockout pick is a real forecast. The model roughly ties a plain Elo baseline on accuracy and earns its keep on calibration. Shootouts are close to coin flips, and the model treats them that way.
